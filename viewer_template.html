Auto-trace the hall from its drawings

Choose the plan and the longitudinal section (PDF from CAD works best; PNG or JPG also work, less precisely). Claude reads each sheet: orientation, room, a few dimensions for the scale, then the room outline and the floor and ceiling lines. The page snaps every traced wall to the lines drawn in the PDF. Takes about 1–3 minutes and uses your Claude usage.
